ABOUT EVENT HORIZON

Experiences Beyond the Ordinary

Event Horizon is a place to discover something different. From creative workshops and live performances to food, culture, and experiences worth remembering, there's always something waiting just beyond the horizon.

What is Event Horizon?

Event Horizon brings together a collection of events and experiences designed to give people something to look forward to. Whether you're trying something completely new, heading out with friends, or simply looking for something different to do, our events are built around discovering new experiences.

Our events range from relaxed evenings and creative activities to food, music, and more unusual experiences. There's no single definition of what belongs here. If it's worth experiencing, it belongs on the horizon.
